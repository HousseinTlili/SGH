DIAGNOSTIC PATHOLOGY

High-Throughput Laboratory Work Queue

Laboratory technicians process pending test orders automatically prioritized with URGENT orders first. Analyzer readings are validated against biological thresholds with automated flag computation.

  • check_circle Automated Reference Flags: Calculates NORMAL, HIGH, LOW, or CRITICAL flags using biological catalogue thresholds.
  • check_circle Safety Confirmation Gates: Prompts technicians with an explicit confirmation dialog before validating critical results.
  • check_circle Asynchronous Result Delivery: Publishes lab.result.ready back to the attending doctor's consultation record.

MEDICATION DISPENSARY

Closed-Loop Pharmacy & Inventory Control

Hospital pharmacists review electronic prescriptions directly from consultation rooms. Medication dispensing executes in atomic transactions with explicit row locking to eliminate stock inconsistencies.

  • check_circle Atomic Stock Deduction: Deducts medication inventory and records dispensing audit lines simultaneously.
  • check_circle Automated Reorder Alerts: Automatically surfaces medications with stock at or below critical thresholds (PHA-8).
  • check_circle Autonomous Resilience: Continues dispensing from local prescription copies even during Consultation service downtime.

ARCHITECTURE & TELEMETRY

Decoupled Polyglot Microservices

Six independent services behind one reactive Spring Cloud Gateway, authenticated by Keycloak OIDC, decoupled by a RabbitMQ topic exchange, and backed by isolated PostgreSQL databases.
